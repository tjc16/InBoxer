🔑 Secure access in a couple of minutes

How to get an app password

Most providers (Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, iCloud…) won't let an app sign in with your normal password once 2-factor is on. Instead you create a separate, single-purpose app password — a one-off code you paste into InBoxer.

🛡️
Safer than your real passwordIt only grants mail access — your account login stays private.
↩️
Revoke it anytimeDelete the app password in your provider's settings and it stops working instantly.
🗝️
Nothing is storedInBoxer holds it in memory for the session only, then forgets it.

Pick your email provider for step-by-step instructions:

📨Gmail / Google Workspaceimap.gmail.com
  1. Open your Google Account and go to Security (myaccount.google.com).
  2. Under "How you sign in to Google", turn on 2-Step Verification if it isn't already — app passwords only exist once it's on.
  3. Open the App passwords page using the button below. You may be asked to sign in again.
  4. Type a name like InBoxer and click Create.
  5. Google shows a 16-character code. Copy it (the spaces don't matter) and paste it into InBoxer's password field.
Open Gmail app passwords ↗

Don't see "App passwords"? Make sure 2-Step Verification is on first. Work/school accounts may have it disabled by an admin.

📧Outlook / Hotmail / LiveSign in with Microsoft

Microsoft turned off password sign-in (including app passwords) for personal Outlook, Hotmail and Live accounts in 2026. There's no password or app password to create — you sign in directly with Microsoft, who asks you to approve InBoxer's access.

  1. Click Sign in with Microsoft below (or on the connect screen).
  2. A Microsoft window opens — sign in and complete your 2-factor prompt as usual.
  3. Review what InBoxer is asking for (read your mail, organise folders) and click Accept.
  4. The window closes and InBoxer starts scanning automatically — no password needed.

You can sign in with Microsoft from the Connect inbox screen. (Work/school accounts may need an administrator to approve access.)

💌Yahoo & AOL Mailimap.mail.yahoo.com
  1. Sign in and open Account security (login.yahoo.com/account/security — for AOL use login.aol.com/account/security).
  2. Turn on Two-step verification if needed.
  3. Click Generate app password (or "Manage app passwords").
  4. Choose "Other app", name it InBoxer, then click Generate.
  5. Copy the password shown and paste it into InBoxer.
Open Yahoo app passwords ↗

AOL works the same way at login.aol.com. Its IMAP host is imap.aol.com.

🍏iCloud / Apple Mailimap.mail.me.com
  1. Sign in at account.apple.com with your Apple ID.
  2. In Sign-In and Security, make sure Two-Factor Authentication is on — it's required.
  3. Select App-Specific Passwords, then the "+" to generate one.
  4. Name it InBoxer and confirm with your Apple ID password.
  5. Copy the password (format xxxx-xxxx-xxxx-xxxx) and paste it into InBoxer.
Open Apple ID settings ↗
🦘Telstra Bigpond (hi mum)imap.telstra.com

Bigpond works a little differently — it doesn't issue Google-style app passwords. You sign in with your email address and your dedicated email password (not your Telstra ID login).

  1. Sign in to My Telstra (telstra.com.au) or the Telstra Mail webmail.
  2. Make sure you know your mailbox's email password. If you only have a Telstra ID, set or reset the dedicated email password in your email settings.
  3. Bigpond isn't auto-detected, so in InBoxer open Advanced and enter the host manually: imap.telstra.com, port 993.
  4. Use your email address and that email password to connect.
Telstra email help ↗
⚡Fastmailimap.fastmail.com
  1. Sign in and go to Settings → Privacy & Security.
  2. Find App passwords (sometimes under "Connected apps & app passwords").
  3. Click New app password, name it InBoxer, and set access to Mail (IMAP/SMTP).
  4. Copy the generated password and paste it into InBoxer.
Open Fastmail app passwords ↗
📬Zoho Mailimap.zoho.com
  1. Sign in at accounts.zoho.com.
  2. Go to Security → App Passwords (Two-Factor Authentication must be on).
  3. Click Generate New Password and name it InBoxer.
  4. Copy the password and paste it into InBoxer.
  5. In Zoho Mail, make sure IMAP access is enabled under Settings → Mail Accounts → IMAP.
Open Zoho app passwords ↗
📮GMXimap.gmx.com

GMX doesn't issue app passwords — instead you switch on IMAP access, then connect with your normal password.

  1. Sign in to GMX webmail.
  2. Go to Settings → POP3 & IMAP.
  3. Tick Enable access to this account via POP3 and IMAP, and save.
  4. Connect InBoxer using your normal GMX password.
Open GMX ↗

Another provider?

Most other IMAP providers either accept your normal password or have an "app password" / "third-party app access" setting — search "<your provider> IMAP app password". If InBoxer can't auto-detect your provider, open Advanced on the connect screen and enter the IMAP host and port (usually 993) yourself.
