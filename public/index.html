📭

We never read your emails

InBoxer only ever asks your mail server for message headers — the sender, subject and date. The actual contents of your emails are never downloaded, full stop.

🗝️

Nothing is stored

Your password lives in memory for the session only — never written to disk, never logged, and wiped the moment you leave. We couldn't keep it if we wanted to.

🧠

No AI, no third parties

Sorting is plain, transparent rules — no AI, no API key, no outside services. Your mail is never sold and never sent anywhere but your own provider.
